# Family cancellation acceptance

This slice covers a guardian cancelling one booked lesson for a linked swimmer. It applies to flexible bookings and individual occurrences in fixed and renewed enrolments. Attendance outcomes, school cancellation, substitution, refunds and dedicated make-up scheduling are separate workflows. A guardian can use an available retained credit through the existing booking flow, subject to the usual eligibility and validity rules.

## Confirmed policy and current behavior

- Early and late cancellation each retain the credit by default. The school can configure the two rules independently and override them per package offer. Use the policy recorded on the package funding the booking, with the original booking ID distinguishing which reservation is released. Later changes to school or offer policy apply prospectively.
- Bookings currently support only the reserved status. Capacity, overlap and roster queries count reserved bookings. A cancellation needs a durable terminal state visible in history and excluded from active capacity, overlap, roster and attendance operations. Preserve the booking ID, original request and package, enrolment, purchase and ledger links.
- Expiry operates on available balances and preserves existing reservations. Evaluate release using the package's expiry date in Hong Kong. The expiry date itself remains valid. After that date, route a retained credit into expired history instead of increasing the available balance. Preserve the existing scoped recovery workflow and its checks.
- Explicit renewal transfers only available credits from the selected source package when carry-forward is enabled. It leaves every reserved credit on the source package. A cancellation releases its credit according to that package, even when the enrolment already has a renewal.
- Lesson-change approval in PR #25 moves the same reservation and protects the reviewed replacement. A pending request leaves the original seat and credit reserved. Existing booking confirmations are in-app history. They currently do not generate teaching email.

## Acceptance criteria

| Scenario | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Guardian reviews and confirms | A guardian sees the specific swimmer, current lesson and Hong Kong time, early or late classification and exact credit disposition before confirming. Recheck the current booking, scope, policy, timing and expiry under the transaction locks. If the lesson or outcome changed after review, show the updated details and require another confirmation. |
| Cancel and retain, package current | Move exactly one unit from reserved to available in the same package. Preserve the original expiry date. The family sees the returned unit and its validity. A subsequent booking still checks class capacity, overlap, level and package coverage. |
| Cancel and retain, package expired | Move exactly one unit from reserved to expired in the original package. Show the expired disposition explicitly. Replaying the request or running expiry again cannot increase the expired balance again. Disabled expiry uses the available disposition. |
| Cancel without retention | Move exactly one reserved unit to consumed. Show that the credit was used under the applicable early or late policy. Keep the cancellation outcome and reason distinct from attendance in family and staff records. |
| Free a place | Remove the cancelled booking from active rosters immediately on commit. Another swimmer can book the released place through the ordinary booking flow. Preserve the cancelled booking in scoped history. The other occurrences of a fixed enrolment, including those after renewal, retain their original seats and credits. |
| Cancellation and concurrent writes | A cancellation that wins against a booking approval cannot leave an approvable pending request or a seat in the replacement class. If approval wins and changes the reviewed lesson, the stale cancellation must require review of the new lesson. An attendance outcome and cancellation cannot both consume the same reservation. Serialize concurrent writes through the existing booking and credit locks. |
| Cancellation and renewal | If cancellation commits before a renewal's allocation, a released valid unit can be carried forward when enabled and eligible. If renewal commits first, the released unit stays on the original package. The same unit cannot be carried forward and remain available, or be counted as available after expiry. |
| Authorization and replay | Only a currently linked guardian can cancel through the family flow. A student can see their own cancellation history. Coaches see only their assigned class roster, and administrators can inspect the school audit. Test direct attempts by clients lacking access, including revocation before confirmation or replay. Exact retries return the committed result. Reused keys with changed details cannot produce another cancellation. Replaying the original booking or enrolment creation cannot restore a cancelled seat or reserve another credit. |
| Audit and notifications | Record actor, booking, class, student, package, server timestamp, timing classification, applied policy and financial disposition. Commit the booking, ledger, audit and any approved in-app records atomically. Ensure a prior booking-confirmation link resolves to the current cancelled state. Notification retries cannot send a second confirmation of the cancellation. |

## Decisions before implementation

- **Early and late timing.** Confirm the default cutoff, whether it is a separate administrator setting or shares the guardian change-request cutoff, and where equality falls. The approved 24-hour default applies to lesson changes. Confirm the last instant guardian self-service is allowed. Recommend closing it at lesson start and leaving post-start cases to attendance.
- **Pending change disposition.** Recommend closing a pending lesson change in the cancellation transaction with a distinct cancelled state and audit reason. Blocking cancellation until a coach rejects would delay a guardian action and could change its credit outcome. Confirm the preferred treatment and the label shown in request history.
- **Cancellation notification recipients and channels.** Recommend one in-app notice for the linked family and assigned coach, with a deep link to the resulting state. Decide whether every linked guardian and the student should receive a notice, and whether cancellation also needs email. Keep teaching-email rules and existing delivery permissions in force.
- **Administrative exceptions.** The guardian self-service authority is confirmed. Confirm whether staff-assisted family cancellations with a reason or policy override should be a supported path. School-initiated cancellations have separately approved entitlement rules.
