# Scheduled lesson reminders

Planning baseline `55a4a9a`, including lesson-change approval. Reminders concern the next booked occurrence. Booking confirmations and change or cancellation notices are separate events. Implement against the merged attendance and cancellation contracts before enabling reminders for live families.

## Existing behavior

- Flexible and fixed bookings create in-app confirmations per booking and active linked guardian or student, including the booking guardian. Confirmation creation participates in the booking transaction. Scoped history, unread counts and read actions recheck current access. Confirmation links open the current student profile.
- Teaching notification email has a dedicated opt-in and exact recipient allowlist. Reminders need a booking-specific preference and delivery eligibility. Preserve teaching preference and behavior. Confirmations currently have no email delivery.
- The SMTP worker claims due delivery rows with a lease, rechecks current recipient access and preferences, retries failures, and exposes operations and manual retry to administrators. A handoff can complete after access changes. Crash recovery can repeat SMTP handoff, even when there is only one durable application event. SMTP acceptance and confirmed inbox receipt are distinct evidence.
- A pending change keeps the original seat and credit. Approval moves the same booking ID. That ID alone therefore cannot distinguish an original reminder from one for its approved replacement. A scheduled reminder needs to identify the current booking occurrence, including its effective class schedule.

## Acceptance criteria

| Scenario | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Reminder becomes due | Use server time and the approved lead time. A valid reservation produces one reminder per eligible recipient and applicable reminder offset for the current occurrence, with stable deduplication across overlapping workers, restart and retries. Support flexible, fixed and renewed reservations. |
| Current details and scope | In-app copy shows the student and lesson details in Hong Kong time in English or Traditional Chinese. The reminder link opens the current authorized record and handles moved, cancelled and inaccessible bookings. At due time, derive recipients from current active guardian/self links; both read and email authorization recheck scope. |
| Pending, rejected and approved moves | Pending or rejected requests leave the original schedule eligible. After approval, suppress any unsent original reminder, including email retries. Evaluate the replacement using its own start, rule and occurrence identity. A reminder already shown or sent retains historical context, with the in-app link reflecting the current booking. |
| Cancellation, substitution, attendance and other edits | A committed cancellation suppresses pending dispatch and retries. Rescheduling or substitution invalidates stale queued details and reevaluates eligibility for the new occurrence. A reservation resolved by attendance, or whose lesson has started, cannot produce a new reminder. Race these writes with due claims and verify the next claim uses committed state. An already authorized SMTP handoff can still finish. |
| Preference and failure handling | Email requires an active scope, the booking-reminder opt-in and an allowed current address. Opt-out, unlink, disablement and source invalidation permanently stop further attempts for the affected queued delivery. Retrying a failed delivery must recheck current preferences, scope, schedule and the send deadline. A delivery failure preserves the booking and in-app reminder. |
| Worker recovery and scale | Process due work in bounded batches and verify the query and throughput against representative weekly schedules. Retries and lease recovery keep the durable event unique. Keep terminal failure/suppression visible to administrators. Confirm that the job restarts safely and that stopping reminder dispatch does not stop teaching or account mail. |

## Decisions before implementation

- **Lead time and configurability.** Confirm the number of reminders per occurrence, lead time and whether administrators can set it schoolwide. The approved 24-hour timing governs guardian lesson changes. It does not establish a reminder lead time. Proposed starting point is one reminder 24 hours before the lesson and a hard stop at lesson start.
- **Late booking or worker downtime.** Decide whether a booking or approved move made inside the lead window generates an immediate reminder, and how long delayed work remains useful. Recommend never making a new attempt after lesson start and limiting catch-up to the confirmed window. Confirm whether already-booked future occurrences enter that window when the feature is first enabled.
- **Recipients and channels.** Confirm whether all active linked guardians and the linked student get in-app reminders and can opt in to reminder email independently. Confirm opt-in default and applied behavior if a recipient links or changes preference during the reminder window. Recommend default-off reminder email and no retroactive email for closed windows or previously suppressed deliveries. Staff reminders are open scope.
- **Multiple lessons and edits after dispatch.** Recommend occurrence-specific reminders with a new reminder eligible after an approved move to another date. Confirm whether a material change to the same class should permit a second reminder, and whether nearby reminders for siblings should remain individual or be grouped. Agree that a reminder does not replace any approved change or cancellation notice.

## Demo send controls and verification

1. Use synthetic bookings and Mailpit by default. Verify an actual captured message and signed-in link behavior. Keep the external reminder feature off until the named approved test addresses, verified sender and SMTP credentials are available. Use exact-match recipient allowlists, one isolated synthetic role account per approved address, and TLS for external SMTP. Keep credentials out of source, logs and screenshots. Public demo sign-in shortcuts and public email input do not confer email delivery eligibility.
2. Before external activation, test that seeded synthetic addresses, unlisted addresses, disabled preferences and revoked links cannot reach external SMTP. An administrator retry or an offer of an address through the public UI cannot bypass these checks. Filter out historical test deliveries when switching transports. For external proof, enable only a deliberately scheduled synthetic lesson for approved recipients and verify receipt in those inboxes. Record SMTP acceptance and inbox receipt separately, then disable demo sends unless explicitly authorized to keep them active. Keep email content generic and bilingual, with only a sign-in link and no student, venue, package or lesson details.
3. Run PostgreSQL concurrency tests for simultaneous due scans, worker restart, stale lease completion, reminder windows, cancellation and replacement, and delivery suppression after opt-out or unlink, including opt-out then re-enable. Include countdown boundary tests and Hong Kong date display across midnight. Verify existing teaching mail, sign-in mail, and booking confirmation behavior.
4. Run real Chromium for guardian/student in-app readback, booking-reminder preferences, stale-safe links and administrator failure/retry visibility. Use Mailpit to prove failure isolation and deduplication separately from any authorized external inbox acceptance.
